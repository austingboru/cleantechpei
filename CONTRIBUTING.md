# Contributing

Contributions that improve clarity, scientific accuracy, accessibility,
navigation and community relevance are welcome once the public repository
has been released.

1. Explain the issue and proposed change in an issue or pull request.
2. Support scientific claims with authoritative sources and maintain citations.
3. Distinguish established knowledge, estimates, personal observations and
   untested hypotheses. Do not invent data, credentials or endorsements.
4. Keep third-party rights and attribution intact. For any proposed media,
   supply the creator, original source, license and modification details.
5. Use authentic documentary photographs where photographs are appropriate;
   do not misrepresent generated imagery or another location as a PEI site.
6. Check keyboard access, readable text, phone layouts and affected links.
7. Run the project's available checks and build after changes, and report
   what was actually tested.

Contributions are offered under the applicable existing license: MIT for
original code, CC BY 4.0 for original educational material. Contribute only
material you have the right to submit; preserve separately licensed content.
These guidelines do not change the licenses or add restrictions to reuse.
